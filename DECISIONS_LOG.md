# EDISON — Architecture & Product Decisions Log (ADRs)

**Governance Standard**: Approved architectural decisions (`Accepted`) are strictly separated from open proposals requiring explicit Project Owner selection (`UNRESOLVED OWNER DECISION`). Pending proposals must **never** be implemented or marked `Accepted` prematurely.
