# EDISON — Known Limitations

**Honesty Standard**: This register records actual current gaps and unverified boundaries so prototype capabilities are never confused with production readiness.

1. **`[PROTOTYPE IN PROGRESS — PHASE 2 BLOCK 2.1 ONLY]`**: Phase 1 documentation was committed by the Project Owner; Phase 2 Block 2.1 (Express + Vite server, scrypt auth/RBAC, atomic JSON store, QR validator, `/r/:token` redirect engine) is implemented and awaiting review/commit. Blocks 2.2–2.7 (Campaigns, Ad Studio, 16:9 TV Preview, Break Simulator, Opportunities, Admin Review, Physical QR QA) are not yet built.
2. **`[REDIRECT TELEMETRY != HUMAN SCAN OR DESTINATION OPEN]`**: `/r/:token` records `redirect_requested` and `redirect_issued` with User-Agent/prefetch bot filtering and 5-second deduplication. An HTTP `302` redirect does **not** prove a confirmed human QR scan (automated previewers/bots/manual browser clicks can hit `/r/:token`) and does **not** prove `destination_opened` (which requires independent verification on the target destination).
3. **`[SINGLE-HOST JSON PERSISTENCE BOUNDARY]`**: `data/edison-store.json` uses an exclusive `O_CREAT | O_EXCL` advisory lockfile, mtime reload check, `fsyncSync`, and atomic `renameSync` to prevent corrupt or lost writes across overlapping requests on a single host. Horizontal multi-instance deployment requires migrating to PostgreSQL in Phase 3.
4. **`[SMART TV RESEARCH IS PRELIMINARY]`**: Findings in `SMART_TV_COMPATIBILITY_RESEARCH.md` and `COMMERCIAL_BREAK_DETECTION.md` are based on official platform documentation and web research, **not** empirical testing on physical Samsung Tizen, LG webOS, Android TV, or external HDMI hardware.
5. **`[NO REAL PAYMENTS OR EXTERNAL OUTREACH]`**: Payment processing, real KYC identity APIs, and outreach to advertisers or employers are excluded from AI Studio scope.
6. **`[LEGAL APPROVALS PENDING]`**: `COUNTRY_ACTIVATION_REGISTER.md` and `PRIVACY_SECURITY_AND_COMPLIANCE.md` identify statutory frameworks requiring qualified local legal advice; zero jurisdictions are legally approved.
7. **`[PHYSICAL QR SCAN NOT YET TESTED]`**: Physical smartphone camera scanning of rendered QR codes has not yet been executed and is queued for Block 2.7 / owner device verification.
